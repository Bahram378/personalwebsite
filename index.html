FAQ

Questions, answered

Can I switch plans later?

Yes — up or down, any time. Changes are prorated to the day, and nothing is locked behind an annual contract.

Do you have a free tier?

The Hobby plan is free forever, with one project and 100 GB of bandwidth. No card needed to sign up.

Where is my data stored?

Primary storage sits in the region you pick at project creation. Backups stay in the same jurisdiction.

How does support work?

Hobby uses the community forum. Pro gets email support with a one-business-day target. Enterprise gets a shared channel.
